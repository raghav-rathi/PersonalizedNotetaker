LENGTH (MOST IMPORTANT)

STYLE (ASD-STE100)
Short sentences, one idea each, 25 words or fewer. Active voice: say who did it. Simple words: "use", "help", "make sure". No -ing verbs, no phrasal verbs, no contractions, no em dashes. Plain spoken paragraphs, no headings, no brackets.

HOW TO TELL A BEHAVIORAL STORY
Use one story. Do not say these labels aloud.
1. Direct answer: the situation and my role (1 paragraph).
2. Context: what failed, who it affected, what I did not know (2 paragraphs).
3. Investigation: possible causes, evidence, order of checks, and why (3 paragraphs).
4. Decision: what I chose, what I rejected, where my authority stopped (2 paragraphs).
5. Action and communication: containment, fix, people involved, updates to users (3 paragraphs).
6. Result and lesson: measured result, its limits, prevention (2 to 3 paragraphs).
